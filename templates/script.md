## Part 1: what it is

**[The repo page]**

Hi! This is voice-first-demo. It makes product demo videos the other way round: you record your voice first, and the screen recording follows you.

**[How it works]**

It flips the usual workflow. You read your script in a little teleprompter, at your own pace. Then the recorder plays each scene in a real browser, timed to how long you spoke.

## Part 2: voices and scenes

**[Two ways to voice it]**

There are two ways to voice it. Use your own recording as it is, or clone your voice and let it read the script for you, with every action landing exactly on its word.

**[Making it yours]**

Your scenes are plain Playwright code. Add a wait for a word, and the click happens right when you say it. That's it. Thanks for watching!
